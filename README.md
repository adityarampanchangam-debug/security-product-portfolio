# Enterprise Security Product Portfolio Management

## Overview
A product management case study covering enterprise physical security and cybersecurity service portfolios, including portfolio strategy, service development, prioritization, and go-to-market planning.

## My Role
Product leadership, portfolio strategy, roadmap planning, and cross-functional execution.

## Product Areas
- Video management and surveillance platforms
- Access control and physical security solutions
- Cloud-managed security offerings
- Implementation and professional services

## Product Management Approach
1. Analyze customer requirements and market opportunities.
2. Evaluate existing products and service performance.
3. Identify portfolio gaps and growth opportunities.
4. Prioritize investments using value, effort, and business impact.
5. Develop roadmaps and coordinate delivery teams.
6. Measure business outcomes using product KPIs.

## Frameworks
RICE | WSJF | OKRs | North Star Metrics | Roadmap Prioritization

## Note
This is a professional case study. Proprietary customer and employer information is excluded.
